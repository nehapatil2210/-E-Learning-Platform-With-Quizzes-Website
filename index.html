Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started and tracking your progress

Is this platform free for college students?

Yes, all courses, tutorials, coding playgrounds, and quizzes are 100% free and open for academic use.

How do I earn a verified course completion certificate?

To qualify for a certificate, you must mark all lessons in the course as completed (100% progress) and achieve a passing score (70%+) on the course quiz. Once completed, your certificate is automatically generated with a unique verification code.

Can I run code directly in my browser?

Yes! Our interactive Coding Playground allows you to run Python, HTML/CSS, and JavaScript live. Standard math, algorithms, functions, and data structures execute in an isolated sandbox with sub-second response times.

What is the Smart Revision feature?

Smart Revision automatically collects the questions you previously missed on quizzes, along with your bookmarked lessons and study notes, allowing you to practice weak areas before exams.
